About AF

A local firm with over two decades of Baja roots.

AF Design & Construction was built from the ground up in Todos Santos. For over 23 years, we have delivered residential projects that honor the beauty of Baja California Sur — its light, its landscape, and its pace of life.

We work with a small, dedicated team of architects, engineers, and craftspeople. Every project receives our full attention — from initial design through final handover. No shortcuts. No surprises.

How It Works

Your home, from the first call to the front door.

Whether you're building from abroad or moving to Baja for the first time, our process is clear, collaborative, and stress-free at every step.

01

Initial Consultation

We listen to your vision, visit the site, and understand your goals, timeline, and budget before any commitment is made.

02

Design & Planning

Our team develops architectural plans, renders, and full permit documentation — keeping you informed at every stage.

03

Construction

We build with precision, managing every subcontractor and material. Regular reports keep you in the loop even from afar.

04

Turn-Key Delivery

Your home is delivered complete, finished, and ready. We don't hand over a construction site — we hand over a home.
